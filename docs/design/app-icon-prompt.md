# Иконка «Глаз-планета»

Создана встроенным ImageGen на основе прежней иконки MataSehat. PNG в `MataSehat/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset` подготовлены в размерах 16–1024 пикселя; прозрачность вне плитки сохранена. README использует ту же иконку из каталога ресурсов.

## Промпт

```text
Use case: logo-brand / precise-object-edit.
Asset type: production macOS application icon for MataSehat, a minimal eye-care reminder app.
Image 1 is the existing app icon and an edit target. Keep its clear white almond-shaped eye identity and the same centered, front-facing rounded-square macOS tile footprint with generous transparent outer margins. Redesign the icon as an elegant eye with a planet pupil and one thin orbital ring, suggesting a small multiverse.
Backdrop: change the bright blue tile to a very dark navy-teal rounded square with a restrained deep turquoise gradient and a soft edge highlight. Outside the rounded square must be genuinely transparent. Square 1024 by 1024 canvas, tile inset approximately 7 percent on every side, smooth large rounded corners.
Subject: a large clean luminous ivory-white almond eye outline, centered, about 70 percent of the tile width. Inside it, replace the solid white circular pupil with a single smooth turquoise planet sphere, a little softly shaded toward deep teal, with one graceful thin pale mint elliptical orbital ring inclined diagonally. The ring should pass behind and in front of the sphere with convincing clean occlusion, and stay inside the eye outline without touching it. Eye outline, planet and orbit must remain distinguishable when reduced to small icon sizes.
Style: restrained Apple-like icon polish, a subtle glass-like sheen and soft depth, calm, minimal, friendly. Precise smooth shapes, balanced negative space. White eye outline should have the visual clarity of the reference, not become realistic or intricate. Broad low-frequency shading; crisp silhouette.
Constraints: deliver the icon artwork alone, ready as the actual app asset. No mockup, no device, no presentation frame, no text, no letters, no watermark. No stars, nebula, galaxies, dust, characters, eyelashes, fur, human anatomy, busy textures, extra planets, or extra rings. Keep the artwork flat frontal and symmetric overall, not a tilted 3D tile. Real alpha transparency beyond the tile; do not draw a checkerboard background.
```
